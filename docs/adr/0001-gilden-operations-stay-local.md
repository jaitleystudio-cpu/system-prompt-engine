# Gilden operations stay a local contract

Gilden needs language for maintenance, search review, research queue, social drafts, growth notes, reporting, and controls. A runner that posted, sent, hosted, deployed, emitted analytics, or merged when a work item asked for it would be a live agency. The contract keeps every external action at NOT_AUTHORIZED and leaves a report without accepted evidence at UNKNOWN, so local notes can be recorded without performing those actions.

Status: accepted

## Considered options

- A live runner that performs the requested external action.
- A local contract and runner that record the work and refuse the external action.

## Consequences

A later change that posts, sends, hosts, deploys, emits an analytics beacon, runs a live search, publishes, or merges is a new decision. It is not a bugfix of this boundary. Reports still stay UNKNOWN when accepted evidence is absent.
