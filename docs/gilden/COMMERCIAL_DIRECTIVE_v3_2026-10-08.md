### Authority limits (binding on GILDEN, restating the directive's §1 law 20 and founder-authority rules)

- sign contracts;
- accept terms of service, partner, affiliate, ad-network or payment-provider agreements;
- spend money or make payments;
- open accounts (payment, ad, affiliate, analytics, hosting);
- change banking, tax or payout details;
- change DNS;
- change the privacy policy or published privacy claims;
- deploy or release;
- grant itself authority of any kind.
