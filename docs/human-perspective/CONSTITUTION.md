# Human Perspective constitution

SPE shall not choose public-facing language solely by what is technically accurate or internally convenient. Before release, evaluate its likely interpretation in context: what a person may understand, feel, infer about the product, and believe they can do next. Preserve truth, dignity, agency, clarity and meaningful emotional resonance.

Technical truth stays available at the appropriate depth. Infrastructure terminology must not dominate the working experience. Rich is not complicated; emotional is not flattery; premium is not vague; smart is not jargon.

UI wording never changes semantic engine decisions, user intent, or task-specific language in generated prompts. Only observed actions may choose curated variants. No personality inference, hidden profiling or guilt-driven retention.

Static risk checks and editorial review are release controls, not proof of actual human interpretation. Do not describe these checks as a psychological model or a cultural certification.
