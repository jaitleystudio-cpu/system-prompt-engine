# SPE-WEB-03 Visual Direction

## Decision

Reject WEB-02's “Intent Core” as the primary visual metaphor. Its crystalline nucleus, concentric rings, cyan/amber glow, floating capability cards, and generic glass panels read as familiar AI/crypto spectacle. WEB-03 starts from first principles with an original system: **Semantic Forge**.

Semantic Forge is not a place full of fantasy machinery. It is a precise spatial compiler. A raw, irregular ribbon of language enters a longitudinal instrument, separates into semantic filaments, passes through alignment gates, is woven into structure, receives a strategy spine, and exits as a ceramic prompt artifact with a portable `.spe` edge.

## Core visual thesis

**Meaning becomes load-bearing.**

The 3D subject is a changing workpiece, not a mascot or logo:

- Raw thought: one soft, graphite ribbon carrying the user's visible words.
- Semantic separation: six materially distinct filaments—Goal, Constraint, Context, Unknown, Preference, Output.
- Structure: filaments route through smoked-titanium indexing gates and lock into a legible scaffold.
- Strategy: a warm refractive metal spine gives the scaffold sequence and priority.
- Prompt Artifact: ceramic-white planes close around the structure while selected semantic seams remain visible.
- Portable intelligence: the artifact becomes a thin, carryable `.spe` folio—not a token, coin, crystal, or trophy.

The process remains visually reversible: later views may reveal the artifact's internal semantic layers without implying magical reasoning or unsupported proof.

## Original brand system

### Name and language

- Product name: `SYSTEM PROMPT ENGINE` in full at identity positions.
- Experience title: `SEMANTIC FORGE`.
- Hero proposition: `Give thought a structure it can travel in.`
- Composer label: `RAW THOUGHT`.
- Primary action: `COMPILE INTENT`.
- Process vocabulary: `READ / SEPARATE / STRUCTURE / STRATEGIZE / RENDER / CARRY`.

No placeholder initials or invented symbol occupies a logo position. Until a real brand mark is selected, the full product wordmark is the identity.

### Typography

- Display and body direction: a precise grotesk or neo-humanist sans with disciplined alternates; candidates for implementation validation are ABC Diatype, Söhne, or a metrically compatible licensed/open substitute.
- Technical labels: a compact mono used sparingly for stages, file format, and real runtime states.
- Hero is sans-serif, left weighted, editorial, and compact—not a giant centered serif slogan.
- Numerals use tabular forms only where real state values exist.
- Uppercase microcopy is tracked, but never so widely that it reads as sci-fi interface decoration.

### Palette

- Graphite void: `#080908`
- Lifted graphite: `#111310`
- Smoked titanium: `#373A36`
- Ceramic white: `#E9E6DE`
- Quiet paper: `#C8C4BA`
- Warm refractive metal: `#B98A4E` to `#E1B978`
- Semantic filaments:
  - Goal: warm metal
  - Constraint: kiln red `#B85D43`
  - Context: mineral green `#63806A`
  - Unknown: oxidized silver `#8C9691`
  - Preference: clay rose `#A7786B`
  - Output: ceramic light `#D7D0BE`

Semantic color is restrained and localized to the workpiece. There is no generic cyan/purple/neon field.

### Materials

- Deep graphite: matte, light-absorbing environmental shell.
- Smoked titanium: anisotropic dark metal on gates and rails.
- Smoked glass: used as edge-bonded inspection planes, never as a stack of generic cards.
- Ceramic white: low-gloss artifact surfaces with soft edge wear.
- Warm refractive metal: the strategy spine and key action edge, used as a scarce signal.
- Luminous filaments: thin, internally lit semantic paths whose hues encode real categories.

### Geometry

- Longitudinal rails, combs, dies, planes, tensioners, and folded folios.
- Asymmetry and off-axis views produce depth.
- No complete spheres, orbital rings, faceted gems, brain silhouettes, globes, floating coins, or halo menus.
- Rounded rectangles are controls only, not the site's compositional vocabulary.

## Desktop composition: one scene, five camera states

### Act 0 — Receive

Full viewport. Camera sits low and slightly off-axis inside a dark instrument hall. The semantic workpiece enters from the lower-left foreground and disappears into the forge at center-right. The accessible HTML composer is physically integrated into a smoked-glass intake plate. A restrained wordmark and stage index sit on opposing edges.

The opening frame already works as a product: textarea, category/target disclosure if needed, keyboard instruction, and `COMPILE INTENT`. The scene responds only to real text/category/state inputs.

### Act I — Separate

Scroll advances the camera alongside the workpiece. The raw ribbon parts into six labeled filaments. Labels are editorial annotations anchored in screen space with collision-aware leader lines. The user's own text remains visible in the raw ribbon; no fabricated semantic scores appear.

### Act II — Structure

Camera moves through indexing gates as filaments cross, align, and lock into a scaffold. Unknowns visibly remain open spans; constraints form physical stops; preferences bend but do not harden into obligations. This is the central product explanation.

### Act III — Strategize and render

A warm metal spine threads through the scaffold. Ceramic planes fold around it in sequence, creating a legible prompt artifact. The prompt text appears as real DOM typography on an inspection surface, not as blurry 3D texture.

### Act IV — Carry

The camera pulls back to reveal a thin `.spe` folio: intent, requirements, strategy, prompt, and lineage as inspectable layers. The scene transitions directly into the workspace instead of ending on a marketing CTA.

## Workspace composition

The workspace is a **creative instrument**, not a dashboard.

- Center: a large workbench showing the prompt artifact or selected semantic layer.
- Left edge: raw thought and Intent Lens as a vertical material stack, revealed by mode.
- Right edge: Prompt Lens / changes / techniques / artifact inspection on one sliding rail.
- Upper datum: actual compile stage, target, and local/offline condition.
- Lower action edge: copy, download `.spe`, import, and JSON, shown only when applicable.
- History: an opt-in material drawer, closed by default and clearly local.
- Privacy and engine truth remain plain language. Hashes/import counts appear only when produced by the real engine; absent values render as unavailable, never as decorative metrics.

## Alternative direction for comparison

### Semantic Observatory

The same material and typography system becomes more editorial and data-spatial. A horizontal semantic specimen extends across a deep black field. The camera behaves like a macro inspection rig: topographic depth slices, annotation lines, and transparent cross-sections expose meaning over time. It remains one continuous spatial essay, not a dashboard or card wall.

The Observatory is quieter, more analytical, and easier to scan. It has less physical drama than the Forge but gives category relationships and Prompt Lens comparisons more visual room.

## Mobile composition

Mobile is authored independently:

- Portrait cutaway through the forge, with the workpiece moving vertically through the screen.
- Composer occupies the lower third as a stable thumb-reachable intake tray.
- Semantic categories peel into a six-line vertical loom, one label at a time.
- Camera changes become sectional cuts and parallax planes rather than sweeping travel.
- Workspace uses one main workbench with bottom-sheet lenses and a persistent compile action.
- No miniature desktop stage, horizontal overflow, hidden essential labels, or hover dependency.

## Reduced-motion composition

`prefers-reduced-motion` receives the same art direction:

- Five authored camera plates corresponding to the five acts.
- Discrete opacity/material-state transitions under 150 ms or no transition according to user settings.
- No auto-rotation, camera dolly, filament travel, fog drift, cursor attraction, or parallax.
- The semantic change remains understandable through shape, labels, position, and text—not color or motion alone.
- The product composer and workspace remain fully functional.

## Accessibility invariants

- HTML controls and text remain DOM-native and keyboard operable.
- The 3D canvas is explanatory and `aria-hidden`; equivalent semantic relationships exist in headings, labels, and ordered content.
- Minimum text contrast targets WCAG AA; focus rings use ceramic/warm-metal contrast without glow dependency.
- Scroll never traps the user. A visible `Skip cinematic story` control moves directly to the workspace.
- Screen readers receive status updates from real compile phases only.
- Touch targets are at least 44 CSS pixels.

## Claim integrity

The design may state only capabilities supported by the present code or explicitly mark roadmap states. It must not show:

- semantic accuracy percentages,
- proof or verification scores,
- user/customer counts,
- provider coverage counts,
- latency or performance numbers,
- security certifications,
- “private” claims broader than the actual local compile path,
- or “production ready” status.

Real runtime facts such as local worker/WASM execution, offline shell state, `.spe` import/export, opt-in local history, current target, hash, and no TypeScript fallback may be displayed only when their source values exist.
