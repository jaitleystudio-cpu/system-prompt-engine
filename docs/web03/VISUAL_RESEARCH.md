# SPE-WEB-03 Visual Research

Research date: 2026-09-19

## Research frame

This research looks for interaction mechanisms, not layouts to copy. SPE-WEB-03 must remain an original product expression of semantic compilation: raw thought becomes explicit meaning, then structure, strategy, a prompt artifact, and portable intelligence. The two supplied images are treated as temporary mood references for materiality and spatial density, not as product-interface specifications.

## 1. Inkwell — cinematic narrative as a continuous act

- Reference: [Inkwell: a scroll-driven narrative](https://www.awwwards.com/inkwell-a-scroll-driven-narrative-for-ais-most-stealth-player.html)
- What works: the creators wrote a synopsis, three-act arc, and storyboard before designing screens. Scroll is the edit timeline; sections are scenes, and transitions preserve one uninterrupted argument. A static pathfinder provides orientation without turning the experience into conventional navigation.
- What does not fit SPE: a strictly passive, single-click-at-the-end brand film would hide SPE's real utility. SPE needs a usable composer in the opening frame and a credible transition into its creative instrument.
- Abstract mechanism worth learning: design one continuous semantic camera move with explicit acts and an orientation rail. Let scroll advance a transformation rather than reveal disconnected sections.

## 2. Cartier Watches & Wonders — spatial chapters with material identity

- Reference: [Watches & Wonders immersive experience](https://www.awwwards.com/watches-wonders-immersive-experience-for-cartier.html)
- What works: six self-contained 3D alcoves share one art direction. Architecture, light, material, and restrained hidden gestures create discovery without visual discontinuity. High-fidelity assets are balanced against device performance.
- What does not fit SPE: jewelry-like spectacle, ornate brand motifs, and hidden interactions cannot become the information architecture. SPE is a precise instrument, not a luxury showroom.
- Abstract mechanism worth learning: assign every narrative act its own camera state and physical chamber while retaining one continuous world. Use material change to signal semantic phase change.

## 3. National Geographic, Into the Amazon — data is the world

- References: [Gladeye case study](https://www.gladeye.com/work/into-the-amazon), [National Geographic experience](https://www.nationalgeographic.com/into-the-amazon/)
- What works: real terrain, satellite, and tracking data drive the visual journey. WebGL and React/HTML are orchestrated as one system, allowing readable editorial content to coexist with spatial motion. Detail concentrates along the path that matters.
- What does not fit SPE: documentary media, lateral scenic travel, and a content-heavy expedition structure would overwhelm a compact product explanation.
- Abstract mechanism worth learning: the semantic model itself should generate the scene. Filaments, spacing, grouping, and transformations must be data-bound to real categories and states, not decorative particles.

## 4. Lusion — 3D as navigation, not a hero ornament

- References: [Lusion studio](https://lusion.co/), [Lusion About](https://lusion.co/about/)
- What works: the canvas remains continuous while camera movement, typography, and interface layers change together. The studio describes a unified design/motion/3D/development process; the result feels authored rather than assembled from sections.
- What does not fit SPE: portfolio spectacle, long traversal for its own sake, saturated accent changes, and audio-reactive novelty would turn SPE into a creative-studio demo.
- Abstract mechanism worth learning: maintain a persistent spatial object whose function and reading change across the journey. DOM typography should be camera-aware and choreographed with the scene rather than overlaid afterward.

## 5. Active Theory — state-based interactive environment

- References: [Active Theory](https://activetheory.net/), [Communication Arts profile](https://www.commarts.com/webpicks/active-theory-2)
- What works: a state-based WebGL architecture supports responsive scene behavior, while compact navigation remains legible over an immersive environment. Interaction is part of the identity, not a layer of hover effects.
- What does not fit SPE: networked cursor traces, alien atmospheres, chat-as-navigation, and deliberately uncanny styling would imply cloud/social behavior that SPE does not claim.
- Abstract mechanism worth learning: represent product and scroll states explicitly, and derive camera, light, material, and DOM transitions from those states. Never let animation state diverge from product state.

## 6. Noomo ValenTime — premium density with baked efficiency

- Reference: [Noomo ValenTime technical case study](https://www.awwwards.com/noomo-valentime-immersive-storytelling-about-love.html)
- What works: tactile material comes from deliberately baked diffuse, roughness, and normal information rather than raw polygon count. Fog variations and scroll-triggered animation give each scene a controlled atmosphere.
- What does not fit SPE: a central symbolic object, romantic softness, and customization spectacle risk recreating the forbidden generic orb/heart/product-object pattern.
- Abstract mechanism worth learning: invest in surface response and lighting hierarchy, not geometry volume. Premium reduced-motion states can preserve the same baked material frames without continuous animation.

## 7. Supplied reference: metallic SPE knot/monogram

- Local temporary reference: `01a0ba67-81db-7c85-85e5-54739712fb1e.jpg`
- What works: dark negative space, physically legible polished metal, hard rim light, deep reflections, and a controlled relationship between a hero mark and technical wordmark.
- What does not fit SPE-WEB-03: the orbital knot is too close to an orb and cannot become the new identity. Blue/purple energy, serif tagline, chrome initials, and a literal monogram would repeat WEB-02's visual vocabulary.
- Abstract mechanism worth learning: use disciplined specular gradients, scale, and silhouette to make a small number of forms feel manufactured and valuable. Transfer the material confidence, not the topology or palette.

## 8. Supplied reference: Intent Core theater

- Local temporary reference: `01a0ba67-8228-7270-a3da-70c3467c6063.jpg`
- What works: deep foreground/midground/background staging, a strong central theater, product controls embedded at the point of attention, and enough density to imply a serious instrument.
- What does not fit SPE-WEB-03: central globe/orb, card halo, decorative crystals, fantasy landscape, proof counters, and dashboard strips are explicitly rejected. The screen also shows unsupported metrics that SPE must never fabricate.
- Abstract mechanism worth learning: use occlusion, foreground framing, controlled pools of light, and one spatial focal plane. Put the real composer inside the scene rather than below a marketing hero.

## Synthesis for Semantic Forge

The transferable pattern is not “put a 3D object in the hero.” It is:

1. One persistent world, written as acts before screens.
2. One non-orbital transformation object: a longitudinal semantic workpiece moving through a physical forge/press.
3. Real semantic categories determine geometry and color.
4. Camera states communicate process: receive, separate, align, encode, release.
5. DOM and 3D share one choreography, while the composer remains standard accessible HTML.
6. Material craft comes from roughness, reflection, silhouette, and light—not neon bloom.
7. Mobile uses a sectional cutaway composition, not a compressed desktop canvas.
8. Reduced motion uses authored still camera plates and discrete crossfades, not a degraded empty fallback.

## Explicit rejection list

No diamond, generic orb, glowing brain, particle sphere, holographic globe, crypto token, card wall, dashboard hero, blue/purple neon wash, fake verification score, fake semantic percentage, invented user count, invented performance statistic, or decorative “AI” circuitry without semantic meaning.
