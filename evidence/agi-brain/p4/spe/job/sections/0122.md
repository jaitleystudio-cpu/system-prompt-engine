## 13.3 Conditions for Hard Rules Compliance: Free Core, No Paid Expenditures — Identifying Essential Non-Paid Activities

### Conditions for Hard Rules Compliance: Free Core, No Paid Expenditures — Identifying Essential Non-Paid Activities Maintaining compliance with the Zero-Cost Product Law (ZCPL) is crucial for ensuring that SPE can be used as a platform for research without any financial constraints. This section will delve into the details of what constitutes essential non-paid activities and how to ensure they do not violate these rules. #### 13.3 Conditions for Hard Rules Compliance: Free Core, No Paid Expenditures To comply with the hard rules outlined in this handbook, developers must ensure that all core functionalities remain free from additional costs. This includes adhering strictly to the conditions provided without any exceptions or deviations. The key activities involved include: ##### 1. **Free Core Library and Universal ABI** - **Description**: The SPE core library remains completely free for building and running locally. This means no paid cloud services, no paid model APIs, and no additional costs associated with local development. - **Implementation**: Ensure that all necessary libraries (e.g., `jsonschema`, `pytest`) are freely available through their respective package registries or can be provided without any licensing fees. ##### 2. **₹0 Owner Spend Target** - **Description**: The core library and API should not require a paid cloud, no paid model APIs, no paid analytics, and no additional costs for CI extras required for proof runs. - **Implementation**: Clearly document that all proofs are reproducible offline with a clean checkout of the project. This involves avoiding any external dependencies or services that could incur costs. ##### 3. **No Prompt Sales** - **Description**: The core library must not be used to sell prompts or treat them as a monetized SKU. - **Implementation**: Ensure that all experiments and proofs are conducted within the constraints of free, non-paid resources. This includes avoiding any external marketing or targeting mechanisms. ##### 4. **No Ad Targeting** - **Description**: The core library must not include advertising SDKs or user-targeting ad pipelines. - **Implementation**: Verify that no additional services beyond those provided by the base SPE functionalities are included in the core library, ensuring compliance with this rule. ##### 5. **Dependencies** - **Description**: `pyproject.toml` should stay free: it remains optional to declare free/open dependencies unless a true local dependency is truly required. - **Implementation**: Ensure that all required dependencies are declared and provided through their respective package registries or as part of the base SPE functionalities. ##### 6. **Sprint 4 Network Mode** - **Description**: Validation and conformance runs should be conducted with `network_mode=NONE`. - **Implementation**: This means that any external network services, including hosting or SaaS requirements, are not included in the core library, ensuring compliance with this rule. ##### 7. **Allowed Activities**: - **Description**: All local Python development and local fixtures are allowed as well as any local tempfile adapters. - **Implementation**: This includes all necessary local setup for testing, environment configuration, and any other non-paid activities that do not require external services or licenses. ##### 8. **Forbidden Cost Vectors**: - **Description**: The following cost vectors are forbidden according to the hard rules outlined in this handbook: - Paid package registry deps for core (no free local dep is truly required) - Paid API keys (LLM, search, billing) - Hosting / SaaS required for proofs - Prompt marketplace in core - Ad targeting / telemetry sales - **Implementation**: Avoid any external services or APIs that could incur costs. This includes avoiding the use of paid cloud services, paid model APIs, and any additional marketing or targeting mechanisms. ##### 9. **Proof Posture** - **Description**: All proofs must remain reproducible offline on a clean checkout with `python -m pytest`. - **Implementation**: Ensure that all proof runs are conducted without external dependencies or services, ensuring compliance with this rule. By adhering to these conditions and understanding their implications, developers can ensure that they do not violate the hard rules of the ZCPL. This allows them to focus on creating valuable research content while maintaining a cost-effective and affordable platform for their experiments and projects. #### Conclusion Compliance with the ZCPL is essential for ensuring affordability and ease-of-use in SPE development.
