### 13.6 Conditions for Hard Rules Compliance: Free Core, No Paid Expenditures — Analyzing the Impact of Prior Art on Rules

The Verified Research in the System Prompt Engine (SPE) adheres to strict guidelines that ensure its development is entirely free and costless, both financially and technologically. These rules stem from a commitment to ethical research practices and maintaining transparency. The primary focus lies on the core library and its universal ABI, which must remain accessible at no cost for developers to build and run locally.

**13.6.1 No Paid Package Registry Deps**

The hard rule stipulates that all paid package registry dependencies are prohibited. This means that only free, open-source libraries and packages can be used in the core library. These include `jsonschema`, `pytest` (for local development), and no other paid or commercial components should be included.

**13.6.2 No Paid APIs**

Similarly, APIs from paid LLMs (large language models) and search services must not be integrated into the core library to avoid any potential conflicts with paid offerings. This ensures that there are no loopholes for monetization through prompts or other commercial strategies.

**13.6.3 No Paid Hosting / SaaS Required**

To comply with this rule, proofs should run entirely locally without requiring any external hosting services such as cloud providers or software as a service (SaaS) solutions. This is crucial to maintain the free and open nature of the research while still allowing for reproducible offline tests.

**13.6.4 No Paid Prompt Marketplaces**

The core library must not include any part that promotes prompts as a form of monetization, such as a paid marketplace or sales of prompts themselves. Additionally, no advertising SDKs should be included in the core to ensure there is no ad targeting or telemetry data used for commercial purposes.

**13.6.5 No Ad Targeting / Telemetry Sales**

Ad targeting mechanisms and telemetry data sales are strictly forbidden within the core library. This rule ensures that the research maintains a neutral stance towards any form of user-targeted advertising or data collection, maintaining ethical boundaries in both its development process and final implementation.

**13.6.6 Proof Posture: No Paid Expenditures**

Sprint proofs must be reproducible offline on a clean checkout with `python -m pytest`. This means that the proof should be able to run without needing any paid services or external dependencies, further emphasizing the commitment to free and open development.
