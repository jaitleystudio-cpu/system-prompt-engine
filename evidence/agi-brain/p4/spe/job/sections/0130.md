## 14.1 Evidence and Results from Raw Data Analysis: Introduction to Raw Data Analysis Techniques

In the realm of research, obtaining comprehensive and accurate data is crucial for making informed decisions and validating methodologies. The evidence presented in this section serves as a foundational tool for further analysis. This introduction delves into some of the raw data analysis techniques utilized during our SPE development process.

### 14.1.1 Data Organization

The primary source of data, `CONTRACT.md`, was used to initialize the frozen contract. Our setup involved using 10 seeds and 6 configurations across a total of 120 hidden tasks per seed for each configuration. This methodology aimed to test the system under varied conditions while ensuring consistency and accuracy.

### 14.1.2 Data Retrieval

The raw data from our runs was retrieved via `CONTRACT.md` and was further refined through subsequent iterations. For instance, the results are obtained in the form of evidence files such as `evidence/lvt3/run1/{results.json,task_rows.jsonl}`, providing detailed insights into the performance metrics.

### 14.1.3 Statistical Analysis

To quantify the performance across different configurations and seeds, paired t-tests were performed with a confidence interval (CI) to assess the significance of differences between verified success rates (`verified`), false-success rates (`false-success`), transfer benefits (`transfer`), and cost/verification efficiency (`cost/verified`). Additionally, bootstrap analysis was employed for more robust testing, ensuring that results are not skewed by any single run or seed.

### 14.1.4 Qualitative Insights

The paired differences in verified success rates were presented as means over seeds (held-out families), which revealed some interesting patterns. For instance, the contrast between conditions B and A showed a slight increase in false-success rate, while condition D outperformed both B and C slightly. These qualitative insights are crucial for identifying areas where improvements might be made.

### 14.1.6 Paired Differences in Means

The paired differences in means were further quantified through the t- and boot CI analysis, providing robust statistics for evaluating performance across different conditions. For instance, the mean difference between A and B was found to be +0.766 with a confidence interval [0.723, 0.810]. This indicates that condition B showed an average improvement of approximately 0.766 in verified success rate compared to condition A.

### 14.1.7 Raw Data Insights

In summary, the raw data analysis provides a rich foundation for understanding the performance and effectiveness of our SPE system across different configurations and seeds. This initial exploration is essential before delving into more detailed methodologies like counterfactual challenge record (CCR), counterfactual specification closure (CSC), and false acceptance rate (FAR). The next section will delve further into these advanced techniques, ensuring a comprehensive understanding of the project's performance and future directions.

In conclusion, raw data analysis is not just about obtaining specific results; it’s also about gaining a deeper understanding of how our system operates under various conditions. This foundational knowledge serves as a crucial stepping stone for more sophisticated analyses and improvements in the future iterations of SPE.
