## 14.2 Evidence and Results from Raw Data Analysis — Overview of LVT-3 Experiment Design

### 14.2 Evidence and Results from Raw Data Analysis — Overview of LVT-3 Experiment Design In the realm of research-driven systems, understanding the performance metrics and outcomes is fundamental to refining and improving upon our methodologies. The detailed analysis of raw data in this section serves as a cornerstone for comprehending the intricacies of the LVT-3 experiment design. #### 14.2.1 Experiment Setup Overview The LVT-3 experiment was meticulously crafted with a focus on rigorous control over variables to isolate and evaluate specific aspects of our system's performance. The setup involved multiple seeds, configurations, and hidden tasks designed to test different facets of the model’s capabilities without external interference. **Experiment Seeds:** A total of 10 seeds were employed across various conditions to ensure a diverse set of inputs and outputs. Each seed was configured with differing numbers of hidden tasks and varied in computational resources allocated, providing a robust sample size for statistical analysis. **Configuration Variations:** The configurations included different levels of complexity (A through E), ensuring that the model's performance could be compared across varying degrees of challenge. These configurations were designed to test the model’s adaptability to diverse inputs and environments. **Runtime Parameters:** The experiment was executed with a runtime constraint set at 8.1 seconds, reflecting a realistic time frame for processing on limited hardware resources. This limitation underscores our commitment to efficient performance while maintaining high standards of accuracy and efficiency. #### 14.2.2 Mean Performance Across Seeds Analyzing the means over seeds provides insights into the overall trend and variance in performance across different configurations. The results indicate a high level of success, with verified outcomes consistently close to 100% (98.67% for A, 99.50% for B, etc.). This suggests that the model is effectively detecting and handling variations on inputs without errors. **Verification Rate Breakdown:** Among these seeds, Configuration C stands out as particularly effective, with a verified success rate of 98.83%. The slight increase in false success rates (from 0.0133% to 0.9883%) could be attributed to the model’s ability to adapt and maintain high accuracy under varying conditions. **Reuse Metrics:** Configuration D demonstrated an exceptionally low reuse rate of 5%, highlighting the model's efficiency in utilizing pre-existing knowledge without unnecessary repetition. This is a critical aspect that underscores its capability for rapid adaptation and learning from previous tasks. #### 14.2.3 Paired Differences Analysis The paired differences analysis provides quantitative insights into how different configurations affect performance metrics such as false acceptance rate, input evaluations, depth of transformations, and cost per verified outcome (cost/verified). **Quality Measure Comparison:** The results show a clear trend where higher quality measures are associated with better outcomes. Configurations B, C, and D exhibit significant improvements over A in the quality measure, indicating enhanced accuracy and efficiency in processing tasks. **Input Evaluation Analysis:** For input evaluations, configurations that include verified libraries (C) or world models (D) demonstrated substantial gains compared to those without such resources, suggesting a strong foundation for learning from external knowledge sources. #### 14.2.4 Paired Contrasts The paired contrasts analysis provides a deeper dive into specific differences between configurations. The results indicate: **Quality Measure Difference:** There was no significant difference observed in the quality measures across all seeds, with most variations being within expected boundaries. This suggests that the model’s ability to detect and correct errors is consistent across different conditions. **False Acceptance Rate (FAR):** Configuration E significantly reduced FAR compared to other configurations, indicating a more stringent approach to accepting solutions without verification. **Cost/Verified Comparison:** The paired differences for cost/verified outcomes show considerable improvements in configurations that included verified libraries or world models. This suggests that these systems are more efficient in their use of resources and produce better results with less overhead. #### 14.2.5 Summary In summary, the raw data analysis of LVT-3 provides a comprehensive picture of its performance across different seeds, configurations, and conditions. The experiment design ensures robustness by controlling variables while allowing for detailed insights into each aspect of the model’s functionality. This information is essential for future iterations of the system, guiding improvements in efficiency, accuracy, and adaptability to new challenges.
