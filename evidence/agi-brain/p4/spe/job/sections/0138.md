### 14.9 Evidence and Results from Raw Data Analysis — Analysis of Testbed Performance Metrics for Validity Checking

#### 14.9.1 Introduction

The validation process for contract integrity is crucial in maintaining the reliability and security of digital contracts across diverse environments. Our testbed experiments have yielded valuable data that we aim to analyze to understand the strengths and limitations of our system. By examining metrics such as verified success rates, false-success rates, transfer costs, and resource usage, we can identify areas where improvements could be made.

#### 14.9.2 Validity Checking Metrics

The validation process in SPE is designed to ensure that only correctly verified claims are accepted by the engine. The key performance indicators include:

- **Means over Seeds**: We assess the average verified success rate across different configurations and seeds. This metric gives us an overall measure of how often our system can validate a claim successfully.

- **Paired Differences in Verified Success (t-CI / bootstrap CI)**: For continuous variables like false-success rates, we calculate paired differences using t-tests to evaluate the significance between two groups. Bootstrapping provides robust estimates for small sample sizes, ensuring that even marginal differences are detected with confidence intervals.
